Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you use Maxtraction ("the Game"), a mobile game developed by Melih Yönez.

1. Accounts and personal information

The Game does not require an account. It does not ask for or collect information such as your name, email address or phone number.

2. Game progress

Your save data (inventory, buildings, character progress, settings) is stored only on your own device. The developer has no server that collects or stores this data. Deleting the Game also deletes your save.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's advertising service. To show, measure and personalize ads, Google may process the following data:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • technical information such as device model, operating system and language,
  • your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you grant app tracking permission. You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings.

For how Google uses this data, see: Google advertising policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded ads

On some screens you can choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. These ads are also served through AdMob, and watching them is optional.

5. Device permissions

The Game uses your device's audio system to play sound. It does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location services.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page with the date shown above.

8. Contact

You can send questions and requests to melih.yonez@gmail.com.
