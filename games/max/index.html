Maxtraction

Set up camp in a post-apocalyptic wasteland, keep your people alive and ration your supplies as you head into the unknown. Every step is a choice: one wrong move can cost you everything in your pack.

App Store · Coming soon Google Play · Coming soon

About the game

  • Build your camp. Grow your settlement with shelters, storage, workshops and production buildings.
  • Manage your people. Assign workers to gather scrap, wood, meat and more.
  • Craft and trade. Make weapons and armor from blueprints dropped by enemies, and haggle with the trader.
  • Explore carefully. Read the tracks on the map, avoid ambushes, and search abandoned vehicles and crates.
  • Don't forget to head home. If you can't make it back to base before your water and energy run out, you lose everything you've gathered.
